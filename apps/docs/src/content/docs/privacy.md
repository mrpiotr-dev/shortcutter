This site uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There are no ads, no trackers and no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds, except the analytics below.

## Analytics

To see which pages are read and how fast they load, the site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/). It does not use cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint you and does not follow you across sites. For each page view it records:

- the page address and the page you came from;
- your browser, operating system and device type, from the user agent;
- your country;
- how long the page took to load and to respond (Core Web Vitals).

The data is aggregated in Cloudflare's dashboard; it is not linked to you, sold or used for advertising.

## Hosting

Cloudflare also serves the site, so it handles each request, including your IP address, to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
